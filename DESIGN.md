## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an architectural, high-trust digital environment built for enterprise-grade technology consultancy. It projects absolute dependability, technical mastery, and operational scale.

The visual style is **Corporate / Modern** elevated by minimalist precision:
- Crisp, luminous white and cool slate foundations that evoke clarity and computational rigour.
- Generous structural whitespace ensuring information density remains effortlessly readable for C-suite executives and engineering leaders alike.
- Subtle hairline boundaries and refined ambient depth rather than heavy visual decoration.
- A controlled balance of high-contrast navy typography against purposeful enterprise cobalt accents, communicating strategic intelligence, stability, and modern agility.

## Colors
The palette is structured to reinforce institutional strength with surgical accentuation:

- **Primary (`#2563eb`)**: High-energy enterprise cobalt reserved for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, and high-priority focus states. Hover states deepen to royal navy (`#1d4ed8`).
- **Secondary (`#1e293b`)**: Structural slate navy providing authoritative weight to cards, dark navigation elements, and anchoring graphical treatments.
- **Tertiary (`#3b82f6`)**: Radiant cobalt accent for interactive highlights, key data visualizations, badge highlights, and subtle gradients.
- **Neutrals**:
  - Base canvas: Pure white (`#ffffff`) for maximum contrast and luminosity.
  - Secondary canvas: Ghost slate (`#f8fafc`) for structured alternating sections and full-viewport shells.
  - Elevated surfaces: Subdued cool mist (`#f1f5f9`) for recessed components and segment bars.
  - Border hairline: Controlled slate (`#e2e8f0`) to establish clear geometry without harsh visual division.
- **Typography & Ink**:
  - Primary text: Deep graphite navy (`#0f172a`) for maximum legibility.
  - Secondary text: Slate steel (`#334155`) for secondary descriptions and contextual metadata.
  - Muted text: Cool slate (`#64748b`) for captions, hints, and disabled labels.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy balances the crisp, contemporary geometry of **Hanken Grotesk** for display and headline levels with the neutral, systematic legibility of **Inter** for dense UI data, narrative body copy, and metadata indicators.

- **Headlines**: Set in Hanken Grotesk with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) to project architectural solidity and modernity.
- **Body Text**: Relies on Inter's optimized screen metrics with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to prevent cognitive fatigue when reading complex case studies and technical architectures.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase or tracked medium weights for table headers, metric indicators, and status tags to ensure instant scannability.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a strict 12-column responsive grid framed by structural whitespace and balanced vertical rhythms:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` gutters, and `5rem` horizontal margins centered within a maximum container width of `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` margins. Content cards reflow from 3 or 4-column configurations into 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column single-axis flow, `1rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` canvas margins. Horizontal navigation collapses into structured slide panels.

Rhythm and density strictly adhere to an 8pt base grid (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Section blocks require ample padding (`4rem` to `6rem`) to reinforce premium enterprise positioning.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through a combination of crisp hairline borders (`#e2e8f0`) and diffuse, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with deep slate navy (`#0f172a`). Surfaces avoid heavy drop shadows in favor of calm atmospheric elevation:

- **Flat / Recessed Surface**: Ground level `#f8fafc` or `#f1f5f9`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, no shadow. Used for search inputs, table shells, and secondary panels.
- **Resting Card (Level 1)**: Pure white `#ffffff` surface, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, ambient shadow `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive Hover / Floating Card (Level 2)**: Shift surface upward by 2px, border subtle color shift to `#cbd5e1`, shadow expands to `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Overlays & Modals (Level 3)**: Pure white `#ffffff` surface, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
The shape language communicates disciplined execution through restrained, soft geometric corner radii (Level 1):

- Base controls (buttons, inputs, chips, checkboxes) utilize `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) roundedness to project crisp technical precision.
- Structural card containers, enterprise data tables, and modal dialogs utilize `0.5rem` (8px) corners (`rounded-lg`).
- Status tags and circular indicators can adopt full pill styling strictly when denoting cyclical states or micro-badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid cobalt background (`#2563eb`), white typography (`#ffffff`), `font-weight: 600`, radius `0.25rem`. Hover state transitions seamlessly to royal navy (`#1d4ed8`). Active state applies scale down `0.98`. Focus ring renders `2px solid #93c5fd` with a `2px` white offset.
- **Secondary**: Crisp white surface (`#ffffff`), border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, deep slate typography (`#0f172a`). Hover shifts background to `#f8fafc` and border to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent surface, typography `#2563eb`, hover background `#eff6ff`.

### Cards & Elevated Containers
- Constructed on pure white (`#ffffff`) surfaces flanked by `1px solid #e2e8f0` hairlines.
- Padding adheres to `space-lg` (24px) for dashboard widgets and `space-xl` (40px) for executive service cards.
- Header sections feature clear separation using an optional bottom divider or distinct semantic grouping.

### Input Fields
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, text `#0f172a`, placeholder text `#94a3b8`.
- Padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`, corner radius `0.25rem`.
- Active focus state transitions border color to `#2563eb` with an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges**: Subdued soft-tinted backgrounds with crisp text (e.g., active enterprise status uses `#eff6ff` background, `#1d4ed8` text, and `#bfdbfe` border).
- Corner radius fixed to `0.25rem` or full pill depending on contextual hierarchy.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (`0.25rem` radius) or circular elements with a `1.5px solid #cbd5e1` frame.
- Selected state fills with `#2563eb` containing a crisp white tick or center dot.

### Enterprise Metrics & KPIs
- Display component highlighting quantitative capabilities: large statistical figure rendered in Hanken Grotesk (`headline-lg` or `headline-xl`) in `#0f172a`, accompanied by an uppercase `label-sm` indicator in `#64748b` and trend indicator badges.
